The property

A house that has already been made to work harder

The erf measures 1 592 square metres. The ground floor of the main house holds the living areas, the kitchen and four bedrooms, one of them with its own bathroom and a door straight into the back garden. Above the double garage, reached by its own staircase, sits a self contained master suite: the only room upstairs. Alongside the house, two detached flats have their own entrances, their own yards and their own parking, so tenants and family never cross paths.

Behind the house, a swimming pool and a covered braai patio share a walled courtyard. The services are set up for Windhoek conditions: the property runs on solar power connected to the grid, and rainwater harvesting is in place.

Arrival

Set back behind a deep gravel forecourt

The house presents as a clean white volume with the master suite raised above the double garage and a projecting window bay to the front. Mature citrus and a striking cactus and aloe garden line the approach, and there is room to turn and park several more vehicles without leaving the erf.

  • Double garage, comfortable for two vehicles
  • Additional open parking on the forecourt
  • Established water wise planting of aloes, agaves and cacti
  • Fruiting citrus trees through the grounds
  • Fenced and gated street boundary

Living spaces

Rooms that flow into one another

The ground floor reads as a series of connected spaces rather than closed off rooms. Timber floors run through the lounge and dining areas, a slatted screen wraps the staircase, and tall windows on two elevations keep the interior bright through the day. A second, more formal sitting room sits off the entrance hall.

  • Open plan lounge and dining with timber floors
  • Separate formal sitting room
  • Entrance hall with staircase up to the master suite
  • Built in shelving and a stone feature wall in the dining room

Kitchen

Built around a working island

The kitchen is built around a central island faced in patterned cement tiles, with terracotta floors, a gas hob and a generous run of counter space. An adjoining scullery keeps the laundry and dishwasher out of sight, with a deep butler sink and its own window. There is room for a full family table within the kitchen itself.

Above the garage

A master suite of its own, up its own stairs

The suite is built over the double garage and is the only room upstairs, so nobody passes through it and nothing passes above it. The staircase opens into one connected space: a sleeping area against a deep green feature wall, a sitting area with sofas, a desk under the window, a run of built in wardrobes, a kitchenette, and a renovated ensuite with a separate bath and shower. It works as a private apartment within the house.

The ensuite and the kitchenette

The ensuite has been fully renovated in large format grey tiling, with a wall hung vanity, a mirrored cabinet, a bath set into a timber surround and a separate glazed shower. The kitchenette means coffee, and everything that goes with a late night or an early start, without going downstairs.

All four sit on the ground floor, with timber floors and generous window openings. Bedroom four has its own bathroom and opens directly into the back garden, which makes it useful as a guest room, a teenager's room or an office with its own way in and out. A main family bathroom serves the rest.

Front yard and backyard

Built for the way Windhoek actually lives

The backyard does most of the work. A swimming pool sits in an enclosed courtyard under shade sails, with a covered patio alongside it holding a built in brick braai, a table that seats six and a suspended bench seat. A rock waterfall is planted up in the corner under bougainvillea. The front is quieter, with a covered veranda and water wise planting that looks after itself.

The pool

A walled pool courtyard, shaded through the day

The pool sits in an enclosed courtyard, paved in natural stone with mosaic tiled coping and sheltered by shade sails through the hottest part of the day. A built rock waterfall runs down one side, bougainvillea covers the boundary wall, and the covered patio opens straight onto the water, so the two work as one outdoor room rather than two separate spaces.

  • Swimming pool in a private walled courtyard
  • Natural stone paving with mosaic tiled coping
  • Shade sails over both the pool and the patio
  • Built rock waterfall feature
  • Established bougainvillea and mature planting

The patio

A braai, a long table, and a bench that swings

The patio is under roof and tiled, with a full brick braai and wood store at one end, a dining table that seats six, a bar counter with stools, and a suspended bench seat facing the water. Sliding doors connect it back into the house, which is what makes it usable in winter as well as summer. Timber trellis screens and a creeper close off the far side, and bedroom four opens onto this part of the property.

Income on the erf

Two flats with seven years of guests behind them

Both flats are furnished, self contained and currently listed on Airbnb for rental income. Between them they have hosted for seven years and gathered 113 guest reviews. Each has its own entrance, its own yard and its own parking, so they run quietly alongside the main house without anyone crossing paths.

For a buyer that means a choice rather than a project. Keep them letting from the first day, give them to family who would rather have their own front door, or take the space back into the household. Letting figures and running costs are available on request.

The rest of the erf

Room to build on

The erf measures 1 592 square metres. The buildings on it, the house, the double garage and both flats together, total 416.7 square metres. That leaves close to three quarters of the ground unbuilt, taken up by gravel yard, courtyard, garden and parking rather than structure.

What a buyer makes of that space is up to them. Any further development would be subject to the zoning applicable to the erf and to approval by the City of Windhoek. No plans have been drawn and no approvals are in place.
